**Season:** 2025. **Coverage:** all 30 MLB teams. **Units:** nominal US dollars, not inflation adjusted.

**Payroll:** [Associated Press, “2025 Major League Baseball Opening Day Payrolls,” April 2, 2025, syndicated by theScore](https://www.thescore.com/mlb/news/3256460). AP collected figures from management and player sources. Its measure covers Opening Day active rosters, injured lists and restricted lists, with signing-bonus allocations and certain adjustments for deferrals, trades, buyouts and released players. These are AP's reported figures, not our estimates. The private saved HTML was used for extraction; it is not distributed here.

**Performance:** [MLB official 2025 standings](https://www.mlb.com/standings/2025), downloaded from the [MLB Stats API](https://statsapi.mlb.com/api/v1/standings?leagueId=103,104&season=2025&standingsTypes=regularSeason). Wins, losses, runs scored, runs allowed, team IDs and league/division membership come from the privately retained source JSON. Full team names come from the [official MLB teams endpoint](https://statsapi.mlb.com/api/v1/teams?sportId=1&season=2025). Every team has 162 regular-season games. No postseason wins are included.

**Joining sources:** AP often labels teams by city. The private extraction pipeline explicitly maps each source name to an MLB team ID, then joins on that ID. The Athletics retain their 2025 MLB name, “Athletics.” Missing or duplicate teams cause an error rather than a made-up value.

**Calculations:** payroll per win = payroll ÷ wins; wins per $1M = wins ÷ (payroll ÷ 1,000,000); win percentage = wins ÷ games; run differential = runs scored − runs allowed. Efficiency rank orders all 30 teams by lowest payroll per win, with shared ranks for ties. A simple all-MLB regression fits wins against payroll in millions. Wins above fit = observed wins − fitted wins. Explore Teams filters do not refit the model or change league-wide rankings.

**Limitations:** Opening Day payroll is a starting investment snapshot, not full-season spending. Trades, call-ups, bonuses and subsequent roster changes may change actual costs. Other sources use cash payroll, 40-man payroll, tax payroll or different deferral treatment, so figures can disagree. Do not mix those definitions. Payroll excludes broader operating costs, revenue and franchise value; efficiency here is not profitability, ROI, management quality or wins purchased. Cheap teams can rank well without being competitive. One season and 30 interdependent teams support exploration, not causal conclusions or reliable out-of-sample forecasts. No replacement-level baseline is assumed.

**Reproducibility:** the two verified numerical CSVs and CPI inputs are bundled; publisher HTML and raw API responses are not. `data/provenance.json` retains source URLs and historical capture hashes. `python scripts/verify_public_data.py` checks exported input and row fingerprints; public tests independently recalculate models. Those checks establish fidelity to the privately verified baseline, not independent source re-extraction or a contractual permission grant. The private research archive retains the complete extraction pipeline. See `PUBLICATION_RIGHTS.md` for the proposed factual-data basis and unresolved acquisition terms.
